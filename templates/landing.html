How the agent works

Recovery that thinks before it sends.

01
Detect
Abandoned cart detected the moment a customer leaves checkout.
02
Understand
AI analyzes purchase intent, cart value and customer behavior.
03
Prioritize
Agent ranks the customer by recovery probability against the rest of the queue.
04
Decide
Agent chooses the channel, timing and offer with the best expected return.
05
Recover
Customer returns and completes checkout — the loop closes and logs the outcome.
